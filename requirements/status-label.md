# status label requirements matrix

> Requirement matrix for the safety.viz **status ladder and status label**: the four rungs a chart stands on, the one field in `site/config.json` that stores a chart's rung, and the label that shows it, which the chart bundle exports through `src/shell.js` and the docs site writes into its pages. Not a renderer: every renderer carries it. The rows keep the `APP-TIER-` IDs they were written under in the demo app's matrix, because the tests and the evidence records are keyed on them.

## Requirement context

- Requirement: [obot.roadmap#403](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/403), the status ladder. Implementation: [safety.viz#272](https://github.com/jwildfire/safety.viz/issues/272) (the rung), [#273](https://github.com/jwildfire/safety.viz/issues/273) (the label), [#309](https://github.com/jwildfire/safety.viz/issues/309).
- Moved here from [`demo-app.md`](demo-app.md) in [safety.viz#436](https://github.com/jwildfire/safety.viz/issues/436), with their text unchanged, so they stay when the demo app leaves safety.viz ([obot.roadmap#441](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/441)). The rows about the app's own label (`APP-TIER-004` to `006`, `012` to `022`, `029` and `030`) are still in [`demo-app.md`](demo-app.md).
