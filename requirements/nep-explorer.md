# nep-explorer requirements matrix

> Source-backed requirement matrix for the safety.viz **nep-explorer** (Nephrotoxicity Explorer) module — a Chart.js reimplementation of the original [SafetyGraphics/nepExplorer](https://github.com/SafetyGraphics/nepExplorer), **Phase 1** ([safety.viz#120](https://github.com/jwildfire/safety.viz/issues/120); parent requirement [obot.roadmap#35](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/35), design [35_design.html](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/requirements/design/35_design.html)). Requirement IDs use the module's condensed `NEP-*` scheme cited in the source JSDoc, the tests, and [`docs/nep-explorer-coverage.md`](https://github.com/jwildfire/safety.viz/blob/main/docs/nep-explorer-coverage.md). IDs are the **base** form so each row resolves the matching `requirementIds` tag in the shipped `docs/evidence/nep-explorer/evidence.json`, which the evidence pages look up by exact ID. The `Evidence Type` column routes rows: `unit` → Vitest (`tests/unit/nep-explorer/`), `browser` → Playwright (`tests/e2e/nep-explorer.spec.js`); where a row is covered by both suites the browser evidence is listed. Treat this as a reviewed starting matrix: the rows are AI-reviewed and awaiting human sign-off.

## Requirement context

- Requirement: [obot.roadmap#35](https://github.com/jwildfire/obot.roadmap/issues/35) — nepExplorer migration, Phase 1 (the KDIGO creatinine scatter + stage summary)
- Implementation: [safety.viz#120](https://github.com/jwildfire/safety.viz/issues/120)
- Design decisions D1–D9 are signed (D4–D9 on 2026-07-28). The rows below reflect them; where a row exists because the port **diverges** from the R source, its Notes cite the design section that establishes why.
- Phase 2 (out of scope here): the patient-profile drill-down — five small-multiple panels (creatinine + cystatin C, eGFR + eGFRcys, related electrolytes, blood pressure, urine ACR), demographics, a measure picker and KDIGO reference lines — on the RhoInc renderer-specific dataset with CKD-EPI 2021 eGFR derived and the source's incoherent columns dropped (D1, D9). Scatter time-animation is out entirely (D3). Phase 1 leaves the seam open by dispatching the standard `participantsSelected` event (NEP-SCAT-004).

## Scope

**Phase 1 (this matrix):** one point per participant at their maximum post-baseline **fold change** in serum creatinine (x) against their maximum post-baseline **absolute change** (y), painted over the KDIGO acute-kidney-injury stage zones, with a stage summary table, filters, a zone-label toggle, a nine-line point tooltip, and click-to-select dispatching the shared selection event. Staging cut-points and unit factors are settings. The demo data is the pharmaverseadam CDISC Pilot BDS extract (`site/data/adbds.csv`) plus a synthetic `AKI-*` acute-kidney-injury cohort (D8) — the pilot population alone cannot exercise the chart at all.

The chart is a **screening** display. KDIGO stages an acute injury — the 0.3 mg/dL rise is defined within 48 hours and the fold change within 7 days — and neither the R source nor this port applies a time window; both take the maximum over the whole study. That is a screening simplification, not the criteria, and the clinical guide says so plainly.

## Source inventory

- `SafetyGraphics/nepExplorer` — the original R Shiny KDIGO explorer, v1.0.0 (`master`), whose creatinine scatter and stage summary this port reimplements. `R/creatinine_data_fcn.R` (staging) and `R/creatinine_scatter_charts.R` (zone geometry) are the two files the Phase-1 behaviour is read from; the `deprecated-js` branch is a UX reference only.
- KDIGO acute kidney injury criteria — the 1.5–1.9× / 2.0–2.9× / ≥ 3.0× fold ladder, the ≥ 0.3 mg/dL absolute Stage-1 trigger, and the increase **to** ≥ 4.0 mg/dL Stage-3 rule.
- Design [obot.roadmap#35](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/requirements/design/35_design.html) — §3 (the clinical logic, re-derived, and where the source disagrees with itself), §4 (the mg/dL contract), §5 (the demo data), §6 (module design).
- safety.viz port: `src/nep-explorer/` (`configure`, `checkInputs`, `structureData`, `getScales`, `getPlugins`), `src/nep-explorer.js`, `src/data/schema/nep-explorer.json`, `scripts/build-nep-aki-cohort.mjs`.
- safety.viz tests: `tests/unit/nep-explorer/` (`configure`, `checkInputs`/`export`, `structureData`, `getScales`, `getPlugins`, `cohort`), `tests/e2e/nep-explorer.spec.js`.
- safety.viz coverage doc: `docs/nep-explorer-coverage.md`.

## Deferred to Phase 2

Not represented above, and tracked on the parent requirement:

- The patient-profile drill-down (five small-multiple panels by measure family, demographics, measure picker, KDIGO reference lines) — and the open question at that gate of whether it becomes a **measure-group** option on the shared participant-profile module rather than a second profile implementation.
- The RhoInc renderer-specific dataset, built from the upstream file (which carries the `AGE` and `DY` the pre-v1.1.0 vendored copy dropped), with CKD-EPI 2021 eGFR and eGFRcys derived and the source's incoherent shipped columns dropped (D9). Nothing is built or vendored yet, so design §5.4 is a starting point rather than a plan.
- A third source trap waiting there: `drawPercentChange` plots `(value − baseline) / baseline` — a percent change, where a 1.5× rise is 0.5 — and then draws its KDIGO reference lines at y = 1.5, 2 and 3, which on that scale are 2.5×, 3× and 4×. Whichever scale Phase 2 picks, the thresholds have to be expressed on the same one.
- Scatter time-animation is out entirely (D3).

## Open at review

- **D4's ladder is an agent-derived clinical call.** It is defensible from the KDIGO criteria and from the source's own chart geometry, but it changes the staging a nepExplorer user sees, and it should be confirmed by someone who owns the clinical content before the evidence page claims KDIGO conformance.
- **No time window.** KDIGO's 0.3 mg/dL rise is defined within 48 hours and the fold change within 7 days; neither the source nor this port applies one. That is a screening simplification and the guide says so, but it is the first thing a reviewer with the criteria in hand will ask about.
