safety.viz Nephrotoxicity Explorer demo

Demo and Playwright harness page for the nep-explorer module (#120). Loads the committed IIFE bundle against a small deterministic creatinine cohort, engineered so every KDIGO zone is populated exactly once or twice: a no-stage cloud, a Stage-1 participant by fold change and another by absolute change alone, Stage 2, Stage 3, a participant whose maximum reaches 4.0 mg/dL on a Stage-1 fold change, a participant whose creatinine only falls, one whose records mix mg/dL and µmol/L, and two who cannot be plotted at all. Values are written in µmol/L except where noted, so the per-record conversion is exercised rather than bypassed.
