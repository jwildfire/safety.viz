# dose-journey

Exposure and every dose change for one participant, with any serious adverse events placed on the same time line by day. At most five cited sentences. Use it from the tray beneath the lanes. Do not use it to explain why a dose changed — it will not. Inputs: `{ subject }`. Renders as a light-blue card in the tray beneath the lanes, on request. Refuses with `refused:insufficient-data` when there are no exposure records.
